Payment Information
Payment Rules

Please read the payment process before creating your parcel.

When Your Parcel Is Picked Up

Your parcel will be collected by the assigned SHATHIMART delivery staff. The delivery charge is already calculated from the current SHATHIMART delivery rates.

When Your Parcel Is Delivered

The receiver will receive the parcel at the provided delivery address. The delivery payment status will be handled by authorized SHATHIMART staff.

Payment Process
1
Create Parcel Enter the parcel details and weight. The delivery charge is calculated automatically.
2
Parcel Pickup The delivery staff picks up the parcel. The payment remains pending until handled by authorized staff.
3
Parcel In Transit The parcel is transported to the receiver's destination while the payment status is tracked by SHATHIMART.
4
Delivery to Receiver The receiver receives the parcel. Authorized SHATHIMART staff can update the payment status.
Important Payment Notice
You do not need to select a payment method while creating the parcel. The system automatically records the payment as Cash on Delivery (COD) with a Pending status. Authorized SHATHIMART staff will update the payment status when appropriate.
