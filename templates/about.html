About SHATHIMART

Moving Parcels With Trust & Care

SHATHIMART is a modern delivery management system designed to make parcel shipping, tracking and delivery management simple, transparent and efficient.

Who We Are

A Smarter Way To Manage Deliveries

SHATHIMART provides a centralized platform for managing parcels from shipment creation through final delivery.

Customers can register parcels, track shipment progress and view their delivery information from one convenient platform.

Administrators and delivery staff can manage shipments, update parcel statuses, assign deliveries and maintain delivery records efficiently.

Our Approach

One Platform, Connected Operations

The SHATHIMART system connects customers, administrators and delivery staff through a unified delivery management workflow.

  • Simple parcel registration and shipment management.
  • Real-time shipment status visibility through tracking.
  • Organized delivery staff and parcel assignment.
  • Centralized records for delivery operations and reporting.
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide a structured and user-friendly delivery management platform that helps customers, administrators and delivery staff manage parcel operations efficiently.

Our Vision

To build a reliable digital delivery environment where shipment information is organized, accessible and easy to track throughout the delivery process.
